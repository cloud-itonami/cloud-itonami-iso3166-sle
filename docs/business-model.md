# Business model — SLE

Independent public-sector market-entry compliance for Sierra Leone.

- National Public Procurement Authority (NPPA) public procurement --
  The Public Procurement Act, 2016 (Act No. 1 of 2016, signed 11
  February 2016, repeals the Public Procurement Act, 2004) + The
  Public Procurement Regulations, 2020 (Statutory Instrument No. 5 of
  2020), both read as primary PDF text directly from NPPA's own site
- Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) business/company registration
  under the Companies Act, 2009 (Act No. 5 of 2009, own text: Part II
  'CORPORATE AFFAIRS COMMISSION') -- registration is free of charge
  per SLIEPA's own 'Starting a Business' page (register@cac.gov.sl, 15
  Syke Street, Freetown)
- National Revenue Authority (NRA) tax/TIN registration -- National
  Revenue Authority Act, 2002 (Act No. 11 of 2002), administering the
  Income Tax Act, 2000 (NRA's own consolidated edition)
- Public Procurement Act 2016 Section 36 / Public Procurement
  Regulations 2020 Regulation 75 ('Margin of preference') gate -- an
  engagement claiming eligibility for the domestic-preference margin
  in bid evaluation must, on independent recompute, have BOTH (1) a
  declared percentage within the Regulation's own statutory 5-12% band
  and (2) a declared eligibility ground that is one of the Regulation's
  own five named categories (ownership / location of bidder or
  production facilities / origin of labour / origin of raw material or
  components / extent of sub-contracting or association with local
  partners) -- otherwise the claim is a HARD hold on `:filing/submit`

## Trust Controls

Any actual portal registration or filing submission requires
Market-Entry Compliance Governor clearance and always escalates to
human sign-off. A false or fabricated regulatory-requirement claim is
a HARD hold -- no phase, and no human approval, can override it.
