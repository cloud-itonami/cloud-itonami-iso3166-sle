# Operator guide — SLE

Portal: National Public Procurement Authority (NPPA, nppa.gov.sl) --
electronic Government Procurement (e-GP, egp.nppa.gov.sl) and the
Citizen Portal publish bids/tenders and contract awards; a supplier
must be registered with NPPA before it can participate in public
procurement at all (Public Procurement Act 2016, Section 21;
Regulation 21). Entity: Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) business/
company registration under the Companies Act, 2009, + National Revenue
Authority (NRA) tax registration and tax-clearance record. Engagements
declaring `:claims-margin-of-preference? true` must clear the Public
Procurement Regulations 2020's own Regulation 75 ('Margin of
preference') before a filing can proceed -- both a declared percentage
within the Regulation's own 5-12% statutory band and a declared
eligibility ground within its own five named categories (ownership,
location of bidder or production facilities, origin of labour, origin
of raw material or components, extent of sub-contracting or
association with local partners). Human-gated filing only.
